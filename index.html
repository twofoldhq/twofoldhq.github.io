FAQ

 

Do I need to buy software?

The automations run in your own accounts, so you pay for the tools you choose (automation platform, AI API, CRM). We tell you the expected costs before we build.

What counts as one automation?

One workflow, one trigger, up to 3 connected tools and one clear outcome. Larger flows may count as two; we tell you before starting.

Will it message my customers automatically?

By default it drafts for your approval. Automatic sending only for templates you approve in writing. Anything that talks directly to your customers identifies itself as an AI.

Which languages?

English and Portuguese (PT/BR). Others on request.

Do you handle health or other sensitive data?

No. Please do not share health data. Message us first if your workflow touches it.

What if I am not happy with the delivery?

If we miss the 10-day Sprint delivery for reasons on our side, we keep working at no extra cost; if it is still not delivered 10 business days later, you get a full refund. See the Terms.

How do I pay?

Checkout runs on Whop, in USD. Taxes are calculated at checkout where applicable.
